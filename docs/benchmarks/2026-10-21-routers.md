catalog prices at 0 were run (no money spent). Command, once per model:

    MODEL_REQUEST_SECONDS=45 MODEL_QUESTION_SECONDS=90 MODEL_MAX_OUTPUT_TOKENS=8192 \
        MODEL_RETRIES=3 python -m capstone.benchmark <router>/<model> --seeds 0 --out <dir>

## What limits the free accounts

- **Requesty: one request in flight per organisation** at a zero balance
  ("the limit scales with your organization's balance"). When our client stops
  waiting at 45 s, Requesty is still working on that request, so each retry gets
  HTTP 429 until it finishes. Nemotron Ultra (25-35 s an answer) and Gemma 4
  (14-30 s, sometimes over 45 s) both ran into it. Fix for next time: on Requesty,
  wait the whole question time and do not retry a timeout early (a per-provider
  request limit), or add a balance.
- **xKiro: a daily token quota for free models**, shared by all of them. Our
  prompts are about 5,000 tokens and an episode asks 20-30 questions, so the
  quota held about three episodes. It resets daily.
- **Our client's request limit is per read, not per request**: one xKiro request
  ran 146 s against the 45 s limit before failing. Python's network timeout applies
  to each read from the connection. Fix for next time: a whole-request deadline.

## For later (the developer's plan, 2026-10-21)

nemotron 120b on NVIDIA stays the main investigator. The routers' models are for
smaller jobs in a later multi-agent design (evaluator of diagnoses, critic of a
conclusion, evidence summaries), where a small model is fine and fewer, shorter
requests fit the free limits. They should be measured per job then (for example an
evaluator's labels against the developer's hand labels), not as the whole agent.
Small free candidates seen today: xKiro `qwen/qwen3.6-35b-a3b:free`,
`qwen/qwen3.7-flash:free`, `qwen/qwen3.5-flash:free`; Requesty
`google/gemma-4-31b-it`, `nvidia/muse-glimmer-30b`, `novita/ling-3.1-flash`.
Requesty's NVIDIA models run on NVIDIA's own service (the same one as our NVIDIA
key) under NVIDIA's trial terms: prompts are kept 30 days and may be used for
training.
